Foresight Studio in Practice

A local-first, visual modeling platform for opportunity analysis in life sciences.

01

Visual Node Graphs

Build models as a visual node graph (inputs, CAGR, diffusion curves, logic switches, P&L) instead of brittle, buried spreadsheet formulas.

02

Monte Carlo & Sensitivity Analysis

Run Monte Carlo simulations and real-time sensitivity analysis, presenting a defensible range and the variables that move it—rather than a single point estimate.

03

Side-by-Side Sandbox Scenarios

Test scenarios side by side, non-destructively, and watch every chart recalculate the moment you change an assumption.

04

Local-First IP Control

Keep everything local-first. Your models and sensitive clinical or opportunity data never leave your local machine.
