Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Life Sciences Forecasting, BioPharma & Pharmaceutical Forecasting, Medical Device & Diagnostics Forecasting, and Strategic Analysis in Foresight Studio.

How does Foresight Studio support BioPharma, Pharmaceutical, Medical Device, and Diagnostics Forecasting?

Foresight Studio provides a unified visual modeling canvas tailored to every life sciences vertical: Pharmaceutical and BioPharma Forecasting (patient-based epidemiology, treatment flows, Bass diffusion launch uptake, and Phase 1–3 rNPV / eNPV), Medical Device Forecasting (procedure volumes, hospital capital equipment installed-base growth, disposable pull-through utilization, and 510(k)/PMA timelines), and Diagnostics Forecasting (screening/symptomatic populations, analyzer placements, assay utilization, and companion diagnostic attach rates).

Why do teams choose Foresight Studio for Strategic Analysis and Life Sciences Forecasting over Excel spreadsheets?

Traditional spreadsheets bury critical clinical and commercial logic across dozens of tabs and nested cell formulas, making strategic analysis fragile, slow to update, and difficult for leadership to audit. Foresight Studio replaces hidden cell references with a self-documenting visual driver tree where demographic cohorts, Bass diffusion launch curves, market share, pricing, P&L, DCF, and Monte Carlo probability distributions are immediately visible and auditable—while still exporting clean, multi-tab Excel (.xlsx) reports with one click.

Can Foresight Studio calculate risk-adjusted NPV (rNPV / eNPV) and clinical or regulatory stage-gate probabilities?

Yes. The Strategic Analysis Lab plan (included free for 7 days in the trial) features a time-aware Decision Tree node that models clinical and regulatory stage gates (Phase 1–3 trials, FDA NDA/BLA, 510(k)/PMA, or IVD clearance), branch durations, development costs, continuous WACC discounting, real options, and expected / risk-adjusted Net Present Value (eNPV / rNPV).

Does my sensitive clinical pipeline, medical device, or BD&L strategic analysis data get uploaded to the cloud?

No. Foresight Studio is a 100% local-first desktop application for macOS and Windows. All forecast models, proprietary clinical trial assumptions, epidemiology inputs, and M&A strategic analysis scenarios are stored and computed directly on your local computer and never leave your machine.

What probability distributions and sensitivity analyses are supported for Monte Carlo simulation?

Foresight Studio supports PERT (Min / Mode / Max), Normal, Beta, Triangular, Weibull, and LogNormal probability distributions. Its multi-iteration Monte Carlo simulation engine generates P10, P50, and P90 confidence intervals, probability distribution histograms, and both deterministic and stochastic tornado sensitivity charts so you can immediately rank which assumptions move valuation most.
