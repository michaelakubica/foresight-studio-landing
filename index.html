Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about biopharma commercial forecasting, Monte Carlo simulation, and rNPV / eNPV pipeline valuation in Foresight Studio.

How does Foresight Studio compare to Excel spreadsheets and add-ins like @RISK or Crystal Ball for biopharma forecasting?

Traditional spreadsheets bury critical clinical and commercial logic across dozens of tabs and nested cell formulas, making models fragile, slow to update, and difficult for leadership to audit. Foresight Studio replaces hidden cell references with a self-documenting visual driver tree where demographic cohorts, Bass diffusion drug launch curves, market share, pricing, P&L, DCF, and Monte Carlo probability distributions are immediately visible and auditable—while still exporting clean, multi-tab Excel (.xlsx) reports with one click.

Can Foresight Studio calculate risk-adjusted NPV (rNPV / eNPV) and clinical trial phase transition probabilities?

Yes. The Strategic Analysis Lab plan (included free for 7 days in the trial) features a time-aware Clinical Decision Tree node that models Phase 1, Phase 2, Phase 3, and regulatory approval stage gates, branch durations, clinical development costs, continuous WACC discounting, real options, and expected / risk-adjusted Net Present Value (eNPV / rNPV).

Does my sensitive clinical pipeline or BD&L deal data get uploaded to the cloud?

No. Foresight Studio is a 100% local-first desktop application for macOS and Windows. All forecast models, proprietary clinical trial assumptions, epidemiology inputs, and M&A valuation scenarios are stored and computed directly on your local computer and never leave your machine.

What probability distributions and sensitivity analyses are supported for Monte Carlo simulation?

Foresight Studio supports PERT (Min / Mode / Max), Normal, Beta, Triangular, Weibull, and LogNormal probability distributions. Its multi-iteration Monte Carlo simulation engine generates P10, P50, and P90 confidence intervals, probability distribution histograms, and both deterministic and stochastic tornado sensitivity charts so you can immediately rank which assumptions move valuation most.
